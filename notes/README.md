# Personal learning notes

Use this folder for your own interview preparation notes. Keep one folder per session:

```text
notes/
  session-01/
    concepts.md
    interview-answer.md
    questions.md
```

Copy `notes/_template.md` into a session folder, then replace the placeholders. Commit notes through a branch and pull request so your learning history is reviewable.

Each session also contains `interview-notes.md` with a starter explanation, a two-minute answer, trade-off prompts, and follow-up questions. Treat it as a personal draft: rewrite it in your own words, add the Alex Xu chapter or section you used, and replace the starter assumptions with your own measurements.

## Note rules

- Explain ideas in your own words.
- Record assumptions and trade-offs, not copied definitions only.
- Keep interview answers short enough to speak in two minutes.
- Add questions when something is unclear.
- Never add credentials, private keys, or personal data.
- Keep the spoken answer short enough to deliver in two minutes, then keep deeper reasoning in the trade-off and follow-up sections.
