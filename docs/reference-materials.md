# System design reference materials

## Primary reference

The curriculum may be studied alongside the user-provided copy of **System Design Interview by Alex Xu**:

<https://github.com/aasthas2022/SDE-Interview-and-Prep-Roadmap/blob/main/System%20Design/Resources/System%20Design%20Interview%20by%20Alex%20Xu.pdf>

Use the book as a conceptual reference for requirements clarification, back-of-the-envelope estimation, load balancing, caching, databases, queues, and reliability. This repository remains the source of truth for its assignments, labs, cloud mapping, and evidence requirements.

## Note-taking rules

- Rewrite concepts in your own words; do not copy paragraphs.
- Record the assumption that makes a trade-off valid.
- Record one failure mode and one measurement for each major component.
- Prefer a decision table or diagram over a long summary.
- Cite the chapter or section in personal notes when a claim comes from the book.
